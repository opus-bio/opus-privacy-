Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 18, 2026

1. Overview

OPUS is an iOS application that tracks your biological signals and provides personalized health insights. Your privacy is our highest priority.

Our core principle: Most of your data stays on your device. When you use OPUS Pro features such as Cloud Skin Analysis, selected data is securely transmitted to our servers for processing, as described below.

2. Data We Collect

a) Apple Health (HealthKit) Data

  • Sleep duration and sleep stages
  • Heart rate variability (HRV)
  • Resting heart rate
  • Blood oxygen saturation (SpO2)
  • Daily step count and activity data

b) Camera Data

  • Skin scan photos (via front camera)
  • Photos are encrypted and stored on your device
  • If you use OPUS Pro Cloud Analysis, photos are securely sent to our backend for AI-powered analysis (see Section 3)

c) Manually Entered Data

  • Caffeine intake
  • Alcohol intake
  • Water intake (hydration)
  • Profile information (name, age, gender — optional)
  • Menstrual cycle tracking (optional)

3. Cloud Analysis (OPUS Pro) — Third-Party AI Service

When you subscribe to OPUS Pro, you may opt in to cloud-based skin analysis. Before any data is sent, the app will ask for your explicit consent.

Data we send:

  • Your compressed skin scan photo (no name, email, or personal identifiers attached)
  • Basic device context (lighting score, scan count)
  • Your daily wellness metrics (sleep duration, heart rate variability, step count, activity calories, skin score) for personalized AI-powered daily insights — only with your explicit consent
  • Statistical correlations between your wellness metrics (e.g. relationship strength and direction between sleep and heart rate variability) — no raw health values are included, only with Pro users' consent

Who processes your data:

  • OPUS servers hosted on Railway (railway.app) — receives and routes your scan
  • OpenAI API (openai.com) — processes the image using GPT-4o vision AI model, and generates personalized daily wellness insights from your health metrics

How your data is handled:

  • Your photo is sent via encrypted HTTPS connection
  • OpenAI processes the image and returns analysis results
  • Per OpenAI's API data usage policy, API inputs are NOT used for training
  • Your photo is NOT stored on our servers or by OpenAI after processing
  • Only the text analysis results are returned to your device

Your control:

  • Cloud analysis requires your explicit opt-in consent
  • You can revoke consent at any time in Settings > AI Data Usage
  • Without consent, all analysis runs on-device only (no data leaves your phone)
  • Free users never send photos or health data for skin analysis — all skin analysis remains on-device

3b. Wellness Insights (All Users)

To provide personalized motivational messages, OPUS sends limited non-health signals to our server for all users, including free-tier users. No explicit consent is required because no health data is involved.

Data we send:

  • Current wellness mode (e.g. Restore, Steady, Thrive)
  • App usage streak and day count
  • Time of day, day of week, and season
  • App language preference

Data we do NOT send for free users:

  • No sleep, heart rate, HRV, or any HealthKit data
  • No skin scan photos or scores
  • No stress, activity, hydration, caffeine, or alcohol data
  • No personal identifiers (name, email, age, gender)

How it works:

  • Signals are sent to OPUS servers hosted on Railway (railway.app)
  • OpenAI GPT-4o-mini generates a brief motivational motto
  • Responses are cached for up to 4 hours, then automatically deleted
  • Free users receive up to 1 motto per day; Pro users up to 5
  • Per OpenAI's API data usage policy, API inputs are NOT used for training

4. Data Synchronization

OPUS uses Supabase (supabase.com) for optional cloud synchronization:

  • If you create an account or enable sync, your profile data and daily wellness metrics (including sleep, heart rate variability, step count, activity calories, stress level, recovery score, and skin score) may be stored in Supabase's cloud infrastructure
  • Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (TLS) and stored securely
  • Supabase servers are hosted on AWS infrastructure
  • You can use OPUS without creating an account — sync is entirely optional
  • You may delete your synced data at any time (see Section 9)

5. How We Use Your Data

  • Calculating your personal biological baseline
  • Determining daily mode (Rest / Steady / Thrive)
  • Correlation and pattern analysis
  • Personalized insights and recommendations
OPUS is a health tracking application, not a medical device. Information provided does not replace medical diagnosis, evaluation, or treatment.

6. Data Storage and Security

  • Local data is processed and stored on your device
  • iCloud synchronization is optional and entirely under your control
  • When iCloud is used, data is protected by Apple's encryption infrastructure
  • Skin scan photos are stored encrypted on device
  • Cloud-synced data (via Supabase) is encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Cloud Analysis photos (via Railway/OpenAI) are processed in real time and not permanently stored

7. Third-Party Services

OPUS integrates with the following third-party services:

  • OpenAI API (GPT-4o): Used for OPUS Pro Cloud Skin Analysis and daily wellness insights. Your skin scan photo and daily health metrics are sent via our backend to OpenAI for AI-powered analysis. OpenAI does not store your photo and does not use API data to train models. See Section 3 for full details
  • Railway: Hosts our backend server (railway.app) that receives and routes Cloud Analysis requests to OpenAI. Photos are not stored on Railway
  • Supabase: Used for optional account and data synchronization. Hosted on AWS infrastructure
  • Apple HealthKit: Data is protected in compliance with Apple's guidelines

We do not:

  • Transmit data to advertising networks
  • Sell or trade your data
  • Share personal health data with analytics services for marketing purposes

8. Advertising

OPUS does not contain advertising and does not collect data for advertising purposes.

9. Your Rights

  • Data deletion: Permanently delete all your local data via Settings > Delete All Data
  • Account deletion: If you created an account, you can request full deletion of your cloud data and account by contacting us at opusbio.hello@gmail.com
  • Permission management: Enable or disable HealthKit and camera permissions at any time through iOS Settings
  • Subscription management: Managed through Apple Settings > Subscriptions

10. Children's Privacy

OPUS is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from users under 13.

11. Changes

This privacy policy may be updated. Significant changes will be communicated through the application.

12. Contact

  • Las fotos se cifran y almacenan en tu dispositivo

Al suscribirte a OPUS Pro, puedes optar por el análisis de piel basado en la nube. Antes de enviar cualquier dato, la aplicación solicitará tu consentimiento explícito.

Datos que enviamos:

  • Tu foto de escaneo de piel comprimida (sin nombre, correo electrónico ni identificadores personales adjuntos)
  • Contexto básico del dispositivo (puntuación de iluminación, cantidad de escaneos)
  • Tus métricas diarias de bienestar (duración del sueño, variabilidad de frecuencia cardíaca, conteo de pasos, calorías de actividad, puntuación de piel) para información diaria personalizada con IA — solo con tu consentimiento explícito
  • Correlaciones estadísticas entre tus métricas de bienestar (ej. fuerza y dirección de la relación entre sueño y variabilidad cardíaca) — no se incluyen valores de salud sin procesar, solo con el consentimiento de usuarios Pro

Quién procesa tus datos:

  • Servidores OPUS en Railway (railway.app) — recibe y enruta tu escaneo
  • API de OpenAI (openai.com) — procesa la imagen usando el modelo de visión IA GPT-4o y genera información diaria personalizada de bienestar a partir de tus métricas de salud

Cómo se manejan tus datos:

  • Tu foto se envía mediante conexión HTTPS cifrada
  • OpenAI procesa la imagen y devuelve los resultados del análisis
  • Según la política de uso de datos de API de OpenAI, las entradas de API NO se usan para entrenamiento
  • Tu foto NO se almacena en nuestros servidores ni por OpenAI después del procesamiento
  • Solo los resultados de análisis en texto se devuelven a tu dispositivo

Tu control:

  • El análisis en la nube requiere tu consentimiento explícito de activación
  • Puedes revocar el consentimiento en cualquier momento en Ajustes > Uso de datos de IA
  • Sin consentimiento, todo el análisis se ejecuta solo en el dispositivo (ningún dato sale de tu teléfono)
  • Los usuarios gratuitos nunca envían fotos ni datos de salud para el análisis de piel — todo el análisis de piel se realiza en el dispositivo

  • 写真は処理後、当社のサーバーおよびOpenAIに保存されません

3. 클라우드 분석 (OPUS Pro) — 서드파티 AI 서비스

OPUS Pro를 구독하면 클라우드 기반 피부 분석을 활성화할 수 있습니다. 데이터가 전송되기 전에 앱에서 명시적 동의를 요청합니다.

전송하는 데이터:

  • 압축된 피부 스캔 사진 (이름, 이메일 또는 개인 식별 정보 미포함)
  • 기본 기기 컨텍스트 (조명 점수, 스캔 횟수)
  • 귀하의 일일 웰니스 메트릭(수면 시간, 심박변이도, 걸음 수, 활동 칼로리, 피부 점수)을 AI 기반 맞춤 일일 인사이트에 사용 — 귀하의 명시적 동의가 있는 경우에만
  • 건강 메트릭 간의 통계적 상관관계(예: 수면과 심박변이도 사이의 관계 강도 및 방향) — 원시 건강 값은 포함되지 않으며, Pro 사용자의 동의가 있는 경우에만

데이터 처리 주체:

  • Railway의 OPUS 서버 (railway.app) — 스캔을 수신하고 라우팅합니다
  • OpenAI API (openai.com) — GPT-4o 비전 AI 모델을 사용하여 이미지를 처리하고, 귀하의 건강 메트릭에서 맞춤형 일일 웰니스 인사이트를 생성합니다

데이터 처리 방법:

  • 사진은 암호화된 HTTPS 연결을 통해 전송됩니다
  • OpenAI가 이미지를 처리하고 분석 결과를 반환합니다
  • OpenAI의 API 데이터 사용 정책에 따라 API 입력은 훈련에 사용되지 않습니다
  • 사진은 처리 후 당사 서버나 OpenAI에 저장되지 않습니다
  • 텍스트 분석 결과만 귀하의 기기로 반환됩니다

귀하의 제어:

  • 클라우드 분석은 명시적 옵트인 동의가 필요합니다
  • 설정 > AI 데이터 사용에서 언제든지 동의를 취소할 수 있습니다
  • 동의 없이는 모든 분석이 기기에서만 실행됩니다 (데이터가 휴대폰을 떠나지 않습니다)
  • 무료 사용자는 피부 분석을 위해 사진이나 건강 데이터를 클라우드로 전송하지 않습니다 — 모든 피부 분석은 기기에서 수행됩니다

4. 데이터 동기화

OPUS는 선택적 클라우드 동기화를 위해 Supabase(supabase.com)를 사용합니다:

  • 계정을 만들거나 동기화를 활성화하면 프로필 데이터와 일일 웰니스 메트릭(수면 시간, 심박변이도, 걸음 수, 활동 칼로리, 스트레스 수준, 회복 점수, 피부 점수 포함)이 Supabase의 클라우드 인프라에 저장될 수 있습니다
  • 데이터는 암호화된 연결(TLS)을 통해 전송되고 안전하게 저장됩니다
  • Supabase 서버는 AWS 인프라에서 호스팅됩니다
  • 계정을 만들지 않고도 OPUS를 사용할 수 있습니다 — 동기화는 완전히 선택 사항입니다
  • 동기화된 데이터는 언제든지 삭제할 수 있습니다 (섹션 9 참조)
